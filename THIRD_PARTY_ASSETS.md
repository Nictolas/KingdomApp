# Third-party artwork and attribution

The MyKingdom repository contains game code, project artwork, and third-party artwork. **Making this repository public does not grant a blanket license to all of its contents.** Do not treat the entire `assets/` tree as public domain or CC0.

## Liberated Pixel Cup (LPC) characters

- Imported/generated images: `assets/character-packs/lpc/` and `assets/character-packs/lpc/generated/`.
- Detailed source artwork credits, authors, licenses and links are recorded in `src/character-system/generated/lpc-credits.json` (181 credited source definitions).
- Additional original generator-export attribution is preserved at `assets/character-packs/lpc/credits/human-male-adult/credits.txt` and `credits.csv`.
- Source project and license explanations: https://github.com/LiberatedPixelCup/Universal-LPC-Spritesheet-Character-Generator

**Important:** LPC artwork is multi-licensed, and individual source layers may list CC0, CC-BY, CC-BY-SA, OGA-BY, GPL, or a combination of alternative licenses. Relevant attribution, notices, license conditions and share-alike obligations must be respected per asset. Neither LPC images nor their derivative sprites are automatically released under the license (if any) of MyKingdom's original code. Where a source offers multiple alternative licenses, select and comply with an applicable option. This summary is not a substitute for the actual license and credit records.

## RhosGFX vector RPG demo tileset

- Files: `assets/tilesets/DEMO-vector-rpg-overworld-tileset/`.
- Author: **RhosGFX**.
- Original page: https://rhosgfx.itch.io/vector-rpg-overworld-demo
- The author describes the **demo package** as released under **Creative Commons CC0** (credit appreciated but not required). This does not extend to RhosGFX's separate paid packs.

## Other artwork and application assets

Other files under `assets/pixel-art/`, the root application icons, splash screens, and `assets/buildings/` are not covered by either third-party statement above. Their authorship or generation provenance should be confirmed before assuming they can be redistributed under a specific license.

## Code and dependencies

No blanket open-source software license is declared merely by making the GitHub repository public. Third-party npm packages retain their own licenses. For any reuse or publication beyond this repository, check applicable source and asset licenses individually.
